Delete your HabitArc account

Applies to the app HabitArc: Habit Tracker · Last updated 1 October 2026

You can request deletion of your HabitArc account and its associated data at any time. You do not need to reinstall the app to make this request.

How to request deletion

Step 1. Send an email to experimentwithai@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in to HabitArc.
Step 2. Use the subject line “Delete my HabitArc account”. No other details are required — the request is matched to the account by the sending address.
Step 3. We confirm by reply once the account has been deleted. Requests are actioned within 30 days, and usually within a few business days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Account record — email address and display name, held by Google Firebase Authentication Deleted within 30 days of your request. Residual copies are removed from encrypted backups within 90 days.
Habits, check-ins, streaks, tasks, goals and journal entries Never held by us. This data is stored only on your own device and is permanently removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
Arc Coach conversations Never held by us. The AI model runs entirely on your device; conversations are never transmitted and are removed when you uninstall the app.
Purchase and subscription records Retained by Google Play, not by us. These are kept by Google for its own legal, tax and accounting obligations and are outside our control. Manage them at Google Play subscriptions.

Using the app without an account

Signing in to HabitArc is optional. If you never signed in, no account exists and nothing is held by us — uninstalling the app removes all of your data.
