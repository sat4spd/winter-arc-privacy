❄️ Winter Arc Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

The short version: Winter Arc Tracker does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Everything stays on your phone.

Data collection

Winter Arc Tracker does not collect any personal information. The app has no account system, no sign-up, and no login. Your habits, tasks, goals, and journal entries are stored only in a local database on your own device.

Internet access & third parties

The app does not request the internet permission, does not contain advertising, and does not include any analytics or tracking SDKs. No data of any kind is transmitted to us or to any third party.

Backups

Your device may automatically include the app's local data in Android's system backup (Google's Android Backup Service), which is encrypted and managed entirely by Google under your Google account settings. You can disable this in your device's Settings → System → Backup. We never have access to these backups.

Permissions

Winter Arc Tracker requests no special permissions.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences. Since no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all app data from your device. There is nothing stored anywhere else to delete.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes how data is handled (for example, adding optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be clearly announced in the app's update notes.
