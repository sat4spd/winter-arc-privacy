❄️ HabitArc — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026 · Applies to the HabitArc app (formerly "Winter Arc Tracker")

The short version: your habits, tasks, goals, journal and AI coach conversations stay on your phone. The only personal data we may hold is your email address, and only if you choose to create an account.

Habit data

Everything you track in HabitArc — habits, check-ins, streaks, tasks, goals, and journal entries — is stored in a local database on your own device. We do not upload, read, or sell it.

Accounts (optional)

You can use HabitArc without any account. If you sign in with Google or email, authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, and we store your email address and basic profile name solely to identify your account. We do not send marketing emails. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen or by emailing us, which removes this data.

AI Coach — runs on your device

The Arc Coach feature uses a small AI model (Gemma, © Google, used under the Gemma Terms of Use) that is downloaded once and then runs entirely on your phone. Your conversations with the coach and the habit statistics it reads are processed on-device and are never transmitted to us or anyone else. The one-time model download is a plain file download and sends no personal data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation that your Google account owns a purchase, but we never see your payment details.

Ads & analytics

HabitArc contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Backups

Your device may include the app's local data in Android's system backup (Google's Android Backup Service), encrypted and managed by Google under your own Google account settings. You can disable this in Settings → System → Backup.

Children

HabitArc is intended for users aged 13 and above.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all habit data and any downloaded AI model from your device. If you created an account, you can delete it (and your email) from the app or by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released and announced in the update notes.

Contact
